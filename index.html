Agentic AI · RAG · Software Development

BALAGH

An Arabic-first local research prototype for community report intake, source retrieval, and staff review.

BALAGH separates report intake, local-model proposals, safety checks, source retrieval, possible duplicate suggestions, and staff decisions. Its repository includes LangGraph workflows, while the current Flask application queues model analysis for human review and stores case history in SQLite. Human staff approve, correct, or reject proposals and control operational actions.

On 36 author-labelled synthetic Arabic cases, exact category-set matching improved from 47% (rules baseline) to 81% (semantic approach).

Python · Flask · LangGraph · Source Retrieval · Ollama · SQLite
